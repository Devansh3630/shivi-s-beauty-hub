# Shivi's Beauty Hub

Create a complete, responsive mobile-friendly web application for "Shivi Parlour & Boutique" located in Lucknow.

Business & Branding Details:

- Shop Name: Shivi Parlour & Boutique

- Address: Kabir Pur, Sultanpur Road, Lucknow, UP 226501

- Phone / WhatsApp: 7897179580

- Opening Hours: 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM (Open Daily)

- Theme: Elegant rose gold, soft pink, and modern white aesthetic.

Website Sections & Structure:

1. Navigation Bar: Shop logo/title, navigation links (Services, Cosmetics, Boutique, Contact), and a prominent 'Login / Sign Up' button.

2. Hero Banner: Bold headline "All-in-One Beauty Parlour, Cosmetics & Boutique in Lucknow" with quick action buttons: "Book Parlour Appointment" and "Request Home Tailor Visit".

3. Customer Login Modal: Simple popup form allowing customers to sign in or register with their Name, Phone Number, and Password. Connect to Supabase Auth.

4. Parlour Booking Section: Display categorized parlour services (Hair, Skin, Makeup, Threading, Facials) with price list. Include an interactive slot picker (Date & Time selection) with a payment option (Online UPI or Pay at Shop).

5. Cosmetics Store: Grid view of cosmetic items with search bar, filter categories, price tags, and an 'Add to Cart' checkout system.

6. Boutique & Home Delivery Service: Showcase popular outfit stitching designs (Blouses, Suits, Dresses). Include a "Book Home Tailor Visit" form where customers can enter their delivery address in Lucknow for home measurement and cloth pickup.

7. Footer & Contact: Display full address (Kabir Pur, Sultanpur Road, Lucknow, UP 226501), direct call link to 7897179580, shop hours (10:00 AM - 9:00 PM), and an embedded Google Maps view.

8. Admin Dashboard: A secure backend route (`/admin`) to view customer bookings, manage price lists, view home delivery requests, and update store offers.
